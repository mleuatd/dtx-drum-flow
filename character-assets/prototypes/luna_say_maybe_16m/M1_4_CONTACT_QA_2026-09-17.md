# Luna Say Maybe M1-4 instrument-contact QA — 2026-09-17

## Purpose

This file is the persistent QA checklist for the 1-4 measure character prototype. It records the visual-contact corrections that were reviewed from the 0.5x playback capture and then re-generated/imported.

## Global pose rule

- Do not make only the arm shoot toward the instrument.
- When the playing direction changes, rotate the shoulders/chest/upper torso naturally toward the target.
- Do not twist only the neck. Head, neck, chest, and shoulders should remain naturally aligned.
- The face follows the upper-body direction naturally; no camera-facing turn unless the body also supports it.
- The stick tip should visually meet the intended drum/cymbal strike area.
- Rebound should read as a small return from the same strike point rather than a different unrelated pose.
- Foot actions should visibly correspond to the pedal direction.

## Priority observations from the playback review

1. HH right-hand hit/rebound: stick-tip contact with the hi-hat must be visually convincing; avoid a floating strike into empty space.
2. BD+RC: the right hand must visibly aim at the ride/cymbal while the upper body turns naturally with the action.
3. BD right foot: pedal action should read as a press/release rather than generic leg motion.
4. SN left/right: keep the stick tip tied to the snare strike area; these were lower-priority than HH/BD+RC but were also replaced in this correction pass.
