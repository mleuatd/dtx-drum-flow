## Visual QA limitation

The current GitHub connector exposes repository PNG bytes as base64 but does not expose them to the image-edit/visual inspection surface as an editable image target. Therefore the following human-visual items were **not independently re-certified in this pass**: face/hair/clothes/body/boots/chair consistency, accidental drum/cymbal/stand/pedal contamination, exact seated registration, stick count, and limb anatomy.

Those items are deliberately left as visual-pending for COMMITTED_DRAFT images rather than falsely promoted to APPROVED. No replacement generation or whole-character redraw was performed.
